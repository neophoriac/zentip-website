_Last updated: July 24, 2026_

This Privacy Policy explains how the Zentip Chrome extension and the Zentip website handle information.

## Summary

Zentip is designed to work without sending your Zendesk data to the developer. We do not collect, transmit to our own servers, sell, or use for advertising your:

- Zendesk ticket or reply content
- Zendesk subdomain
- Browsing history
- Notification content

Zentip does not use developer-operated analytics or advertising trackers in the extension.

Subscription and payment information is handled separately by ExtensionPay and Stripe, as explained below.

## How Zentip works

Zentip requires you to be signed in to Zendesk in Chrome and asks for your Zendesk subdomain so it knows which account to monitor. It processes the information needed to find new tickets or replies and uses Chrome's notification system to display Windows notifications.

This Zendesk information is processed within your browser and is not sent to or stored on servers operated by the Zentip developer. Configuration needed for the extension to work may be stored locally by Chrome on your device.

## Optional webhook integration

If you enable webhook integration, Zentip sends the notification information to the webhook URL that you configure. You choose and control that destination. The Zentip developer does not receive the webhook content and is not responsible for how your selected webhook provider handles it.

## Trials, subscriptions, and payments

Zentip uses [ExtensionPay](https://extensionpay.com/) to manage free trials, subscription status, and cancellations. ExtensionPay communicates with its servers to provide these features.

ExtensionPay states that users remain anonymous until they pay. When a user purchases a subscription, ExtensionPay collects the user's email address and uses Stripe to process and store payment information. Zentip does not receive or store your full card details.

ExtensionPay and Stripe process information under their own privacy practices. You should review [ExtensionPay's privacy information](https://extensionpay.com/#faq) and [Stripe's Privacy Policy](https://stripe.com/privacy) for details.

## Zentip website

The Zentip website does not use Zentip-operated analytics, advertising trackers, or marketing cookies. The website is hosted by GitHub Pages, so GitHub may process technical request information according to the [GitHub General Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## Contacting us

If you use the website contact form, the name, email address, subject, and message you provide are processed by [Formspree](https://formspree.io/legal/privacy-policy/) and delivered to the Zentip support inbox. We use this information only to respond to your request and provide support. You can instead contact us directly by email.

## Third-party services

Your use of Zendesk remains subject to Zendesk's own terms and privacy practices. Zentip does not control how Zendesk, ExtensionPay, Stripe, GitHub, Formspree, or a webhook provider processes information through its own services.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Zentip's features or third-party services change. The date at the top of this page will show when it was last revised.

## Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, use the Contact page or email support@zentip.org.
